Version 3 (2026-09-25): Asphalt Track - Dimitrios Savva / Poly Haven
https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_track
License: CC0 1.0 (public domain)
Diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps at 2048 x 2048, 2.0 metres per tile,
recompressed as asfalto_diff_v3.jpg, asfalto_nor_gl_v3.jpg and asfalto_rough_v3.jpg.
The game shader re-tones the diffuse to a sun-weathered grey and adds the racing
line, marbles, crack sealant, patches and seams (decorative, not survey data).

Version 4 (2026-10-03): coarse grain map asfalto_grao_v1.jpg (2048 x 2048, drawn
6 metres per tile), generated by scripts/gerar_asfalto_grao.py for this game
(procedural, no third-party source). The scan's detail is millimetric and fades
to a flat grey a few metres ahead of the car; this map adds 12-33 mm stones,
voids and patches of exposed aggregate that keep their contrast far away, so the
road keeps its texture at speed. The shader also draws old tyre-mark pairs and
oil drips along the racing line (decorative, not survey data).

Version 5 (2026-10-05): asfalto_grao_v2.jpg replaces v1, generated by the same
script with nothing longer than half a metre in it (v1 had 1-3 m blotches that
marked its 6 m tile as a lattice on the road). The shader draws it with random
offsets and turns over the road and adds the broad patchiness in track coordinates.
